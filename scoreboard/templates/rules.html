EVENT RULES

Play the challenge, not the platform.

These rules exist so a laptop-hosted event stays fun, fair, and safe for everyone.

Do

  • Work with your own team and your own launch links.
  • Use browser DevTools, curl, Burp, Ghidra, or any tool you like against the challenge services.
  • Read the hints. They are sequential and cost points, not dignity.
  • Report accidental access to another team's data to the organizer.

Don't

  • Do not share flags, launch links, or forged signatures across teams.
  • Do not attack the scoreboard, the host machine, the Wi-Fi, or other teams.
  • Do not brute-force flags or flood the submission endpoint.
  • Do not use real credentials or real personal data anywhere.

Scoring

The Intern's Secret is worth 100 points. Chronos Engine is worth 300. Each challenge has three hints; unlocking one subtracts its cost from your score. Your score never goes below zero.

Anti-cheat

Flags are HMAC-derived per team and the platform records every submission. A flag that belongs to another team is stored as a flag_share_attempt and reviewed after the event. Chronos keys are also per team, so one team's forged signature is invalid on another team's instance.

Safety and scope

Every service here is a fictional training target. There are no real companies, people, domains, or production systems involved. The platform and challenge services are isolated to the event laptop. If you are playing remotely, only connect to the URLs the organizer gives you.
