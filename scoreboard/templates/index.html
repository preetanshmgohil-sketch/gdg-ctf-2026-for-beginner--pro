HOW IT WORKS

No cloning. No building. No SSH keys.

  1. 01
    Register your team

    Open the platform on the event Wi-Fi. Pick a team name and password.

  2. 02
    Launch a challenge

    The platform signs a short-lived team token and opens the challenge service in a new tab.

  3. 03
    Submit the flag

    Your flag is derived for your team. A flag copied from another team will not score — and is logged.

FAIR PLAY

Unique flags, without containers.

The platform does not store a canonical answer key. It recomputes GDG{prefix_<team-hmac>} on demand. Chronos also derives a different ECDSA key for each team, so a forged signature is tied to the team that forged it. The scoreboard records every submission and flags cross-team flag attempts for organizer review.
